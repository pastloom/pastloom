Grand Theft Auto VI: The Story Behind Gaming's Longest Wait

This article is written in English. Choose a language to open an automatic translation by Google Translate. Automatic translations can sometimes get historical names and dates wrong — for full accuracy, read the original English article.

Grand Theft Auto VI — GTA 6, as almost everyone calls it — was released on 19 November 2026, and no game in history had been waited for so long. Its older brother, GTA V, came out in 2013, so fans waited thirteen years, through rumours, a famous leak and two delays.

This is the story of that wait — and of the game that finally arrived: a return to Vice City, in the fictional state of Leonida, following two heroes, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval.

Thirteen years of waiting

When Grand Theft Auto V launched in 2013, nobody expected it to rule gaming for thirteen years. It kept selling and selling — Take-Two has reported sales of about 235 million copies — while its online world, GTA Online, kept millions of players busy. All that time, one question grew louder every year: where is GTA 6?

Rockstar stayed silent until February 2022, when it finally confirmed that a new Grand Theft Auto was in development. Seven months later, in September 2022, unfinished footage of the game was stolen and leaked online — one of the biggest leaks in gaming history. Then, in December 2023, the first official trailer arrived, confirmed the return to Vice City, and raced to record viewing numbers within a day.

Delays, trailers and the road to launch

The wait was not over. A second trailer followed in May 2025, showing Jason and Lucia and the state of Leonida in rich detail — but Rockstar also delayed the game twice, saying the extra time was needed to finish it to the level of polish players expect. Each delay made world news, which shows how large this one game had become.

As launch day approached, news reports estimated that pre-orders had already passed $1 billion — a figure reported by several outlets before release, though exact totals belong to Rockstar and its parent company, Take-Two. On 19 November 2026 the game finally launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, and early reports described the biggest entertainment launch in history, with first-day sales reported at about $3.79 billion.

Vice City again — with Lucia and Jason

GTA VI takes the series back to Vice City, Rockstar's fictional version of Miami, now part of the wider state of Leonida — with swamps based on the Everglades, island chains based on the Florida Keys, small towns and highways under a hard southern sun. The world gently mocks modern life: influencers, viral stunts and police body cameras all appear.

At its heart are two heroes who share the lead. Lucia Caminos — the first female protagonist of a mainline Grand Theft Auto game — begins the story leaving Leonida Penitentiary, while Jason Duval, who served in the Army and later worked for drug runners in the Leonida Keys, dreams of an easier life. When an easy score goes wrong, the two are pulled into a conspiracy across the state, and must rely on each other to survive. After thirteen years, the longest wait in gaming was over.

Did You Know?

The wait for GTA VI was so famous that the game's delays were reported like major world events — and its first trailer, released in December 2023, was viewed more than 90 million times in its first 24 hours.
