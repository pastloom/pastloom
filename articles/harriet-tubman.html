Harriet Tubman

This article is written in English. Choose a language to open an automatic translation by Google Translate. Automatic translations can sometimes get historical names and dates wrong — for full accuracy, read the original English article.

Harriet Tubman was an American woman who escaped slavery and then risked her life, again and again, to lead other enslaved people to freedom. She was the most famous conductor of the secret network called the Underground Railroad.

During the American Civil War, Tubman worked as a scout, spy and nurse for the Union Army, and guided a river raid that freed more than 700 enslaved people in a single night.

Escape to freedom

Born enslaved in Maryland, Tubman endured hard labour and beatings. As a teenager, an overseer's thrown weight struck her head, causing pain and blackouts for the rest of her life.

In 1849 she heard she might be sold away from her family. She escaped alone, travelling by night and following the North Star, until she crossed into free Pennsylvania. She later said she felt like she was in heaven.

The Underground Railroad

Freedom was not enough for Tubman while her family remained enslaved. She returned south about 13 times, guiding around 70 people — parents, brothers, friends — along the secret routes of the Underground Railroad.

She travelled in winter darkness, carried a pistol for protection, and used songs, disguises and coded messages. Slave owners offered rewards for her capture, but she never lost a single passenger.

War service and last years

When the Civil War began, Tubman joined the Union cause in South Carolina as a cook and nurse, then as a scout and spy behind enemy lines.

In June 1863 she helped plan and guide the Combahee River raid, which freed more than 700 enslaved people — the first US military operation planned and led by a woman. She spent her later years fighting for women's voting rights and caring for the poor at her home in Auburn, New York.

Did You Know?

Harriet Tubman was called Moses by those she freed, after the Bible leader who led his people out of slavery.
