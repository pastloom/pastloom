Grand Theft Auto V: The Game That Defined an Era

This article is written in English. Choose a language to open an automatic translation by Google Translate. Automatic translations can sometimes get historical names and dates wrong — for full accuracy, read the original English article.

Grand Theft Auto V, released on 17 September 2013, is the game that defined a whole era of gaming. Set in Los Santos, a fictional version of Los Angeles, it follows three very different criminals whose lives crash into each other.

Most games fade after a year or two. GTA V stayed at the top for thirteen years, across three generations of consoles, until GTA VI finally took its crown in November 2026.

Three criminals, one city

GTA V did something no GTA had done: it gave players three heroes instead of one. Michael is a retired bank robber living in luxury and misery with his family. Franklin is a young man from the streets, hungry for a bigger life. Trevor, Michael's old partner, is a wild storm of chaos living in the desert.

The story throws the three together for a series of daring heists — robberies the player plans and carries out, switching between heroes in the middle of the action. Around them stretched Los Santos: a shining, funny and cruel copy of Los Angeles, with beaches, mountains, deserts and a whole countryside to explore.

The game that would not grow old

GTA V was reported to have earned $1 billion in its first three days. But its true wonder was what happened next: it simply refused to fade. Rockstar re-released it for each new generation of consoles — 2014, then again in 2022 — and each time, new players bought it by the million.

The engine of that long life was GTA Online, launched in October 2013. Year after year of free updates — heists, businesses, nightclubs, new islands and stories — turned one game into a living world that millions called home. Our separate article, The History of GTA Online, tells that story in full.

The legacy of an era

For thirteen years, GTA V was the mountain every other game was measured against. It proved that a single game world, cared for and expanded, could outlive whole consoles. It also created something new: a thirteen-year wait for a sequel, which made Grand Theft Auto VI the most anticipated game in history.

When GTA VI launched on 19 November 2026, GTA V did not disappear — millions still play it and its online world. But the era had a new king. GTA V's place in history is secure: the best-selling game of its generation, and the bridge between the old gaming world and the new.

Did You Know?

GTA V has been released on three different generations of consoles — PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5, and their Xbox partners — something almost no other game has achieved while staying near the top of the sales charts the whole time.
