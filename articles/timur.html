Timur

This article is written in English. Choose a language to open an automatic translation by Google Translate. Automatic translations can sometimes get historical names and dates wrong — for full accuracy, read the original English article.

Timur, known in Europe as Tamerlane, was a conqueror from Central Asia who built one of the largest empires in history in the late 1300s. From his capital at Samarkand, his armies marched from Turkey to India.

Timur was as cruel in war as he was brilliant, and cities that resisted him were destroyed. Yet he also made Samarkand a jewel of art and architecture, and his descendants later founded the Mughal Empire in India.

A lame shepherd's son rises

Timur was born near Samarkand into a minor clan of Mongol-Turkic people. An arrow wound left him lame in one leg and with a stiff arm — hence his nickname, Timur the Lame.

He was also a superb horseman, a chess player, and a master of alliances and betrayal. By 1370 he had seized Samarkand and begun the conquests that would shake Asia for thirty-five years.

Conquests across Asia

Timur conquered Persia, Iraq and the Caucasus, then smashed the Golden Horde in Russia. In 1398 he invaded India and took Delhi; in 1402, at Ankara, he defeated and captured the Ottoman sultan Bayezid — the only Ottoman sultan ever taken prisoner in battle.

His wars killed enormous numbers of people, and he raised towers of skulls outside defeated cities. No general of his age defeated him.

Samarkand the beautiful

The wealth of conquered lands flowed into Samarkand. Timur filled it with mosques, gardens and the great Bibi Khanum mosque, and brought in artists and craftsmen from every land he took.

His empire did not long survive him, but his family — the Timurids — later produced a golden age of art and astronomy, and his descendant Babur founded the Mughal Empire in India in 1526.

Did You Know?

Timur loved chess so much that he named one of his sons Shah Rukh — after a chess move involving the rook, which was being played when news of the boy's birth arrived.
