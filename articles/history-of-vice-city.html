The History of Vice City: From the 1980s to GTA VI

This article is written in English. Choose a language to open an automatic translation by Google Translate. Automatic translations can sometimes get historical names and dates wrong — for full accuracy, read the original English article.

Vice City is a fictional city — Rockstar Games' sun-drenched version of Miami, Florida. It first appeared in the original Grand Theft Auto in 1997, became a star in 2002 with a game set in the neon 1980s, and returned in the modern day in Grand Theft Auto VI in 2026.

Few made-up places are loved like Vice City. This is the story of how a few streets seen from above grew into a whole state — Leonida — and a second home for millions of players.

Born in 1997, made immortal in 1986

The first Vice City of 1997 was a small map of streets seen from directly above. The city became a legend five years later, when Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002) rebuilt it in 3D and set its story in 1986 — the age of pastel suits, speedboats and synthesizer pop.

Players followed Tommy Vercetti, a mobster fresh out of prison, as he built a criminal empire under the palm trees. The game's look and mood borrowed lovingly from the television show Miami Vice and the film Scarface, and its radio stations played real 1980s hits. For a generation of players, Vice City was not just a map — it was a holiday in 1986 that they never forgot. A prequel, Vice City Stories (2006), returned to the city in 1984.

The real Miami inside the fiction

Vice City works because Miami is inside it. The pastel-coloured Art Deco hotels of Ocean Beach copy Miami Beach's real Ocean Drive. The beaches, marinas, golf islands and swampy edges all come from South Florida — squeezed closer together, brightened, and filled with jokes.

Rockstar's method, here and in Liberty City (New York) and Los Santos (Los Angeles), is to build a near-copy rather than use the real name. That freedom lets the makers pack a city's whole spirit — its music, weather, fashions and troubles — into one place that players can learn street by street.

Homecoming: Vice City in GTA VI

Grand Theft Auto VI, released on 19 November 2026, brings Vice City into the present day — a city of influencers, viral videos and police body cameras — and for the first time surrounds it with a whole state: Leonida, based on Florida. Beyond the city lie the Grassrivers, wetlands based on the Everglades; the Leonida Keys, based on the Florida Keys; and towns such as Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia.

Long-time players returned to a city they had last seen in 1986, now grown older along with them. New players met it for the first time. Either way, Vice City's third era had begun — proof that a fictional place, drawn with love, can feel as real as anywhere on Earth.

Did You Know?

Vice City has now starred in games set in three different eras — the 1990s city seen from above in 1997, the neon year 1986 in the 2002 game, and the social-media present day in GTA VI — so players can watch the same city grow across nearly thirty real years.
