The History of GTA Online

This article is written in English. Choose a language to open an automatic translation by Google Translate. Automatic translations can sometimes get historical names and dates wrong — for full accuracy, read the original English article.

GTA Online launched on 1 October 2013, two weeks after Grand Theft Auto V, as a free online world included with the game. Rockstar expected it to be a bonus. Instead, it became one of the most successful online games in history.

For thirteen years, GTA Online's updates kept Los Santos alive, kept GTA V selling, and carried the whole series through the long wait for GTA VI.

A second game hidden inside GTA V

GTA Online opened its servers on 1 October 2013, and so many players rushed in that the first weeks were famously rocky, with crowded servers and lost characters. Rockstar repaired and expanded it, and a pattern was set: this was not a side mode but a second, living game, where each player built their own criminal career in a Los Santos shared with others.

Players bought apartments, garages, businesses and nightclubs, raced, fought, and simply wandered together. A huge role-playing community grew up too, with players acting out everyday jobs and lives inside the city on special servers, watched by millions on streaming sites.

Heists, businesses and a world that kept growing

The turning point came in 2015 with Heists: long, multi-part robberies that teams of four planned and carried out together, like the great heist stories of GTA V itself — but played with friends. After that, the updates never really stopped: biker gangs, gun-running, nightclubs, casinos, a music-filled island expansion, and new story missions year after year.

Because the world kept growing, GTA V kept selling — an almost unheard-of thirteen years at the top of the charts. In March 2022, GTA Online even stepped out on its own, released as a standalone game for the newest consoles, no longer just a passenger inside GTA V.

The bridge to GTA VI

GTA Online's deepest role in history may be this: it carried Grand Theft Auto through the longest gap between main games. While Rockstar worked in silence on GTA VI, Los Santos stayed open every day, and a whole generation of players grew up inside it.

When Grand Theft Auto VI launched on 19 November 2026 — a single-player story of Lucia and Jason — GTA Online was still running, and millions of players had one foot in each era. Whatever Rockstar builds online next, it will stand on the world that began, almost by accident, in October 2013.

Did You Know?

GTA Online was nearly overwhelmed at birth: so many players tried to enter Los Santos in October 2013 that Rockstar had to apologise for the broken first weeks — and gave every player free in-game money as compensation.
