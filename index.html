About Pastloom

Two kinds of articles live here. Our Library articles are Pastloom’s own — prepared for this site in simple English, researched from published sources and checked before publishing, and marked OUR ARTICLE. External Source articles are drawn live from Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia written by volunteers around the world, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license — each one links back to its original on Wikipedia, where you can see its authors and history.

This is a history website: every topic here is history or a part of history — people and heroes, countries, wars, civilisations, empires, the history of science, animals in history, inventions and everyday life in the past.
